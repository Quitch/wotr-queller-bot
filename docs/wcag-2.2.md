## Accepted deviations

- **3.1.5 Reading level and 3.1.6 Pronunciation.** The prompts, actions and card text are the Queller Bot's and the game's own wording, reproduced as written so that they match the paper flowcharts and cards the player has on the table; a lower-secondary rewrite would not match them. Mitigation: every game term links to its glossary definition, and Help explains the app in plain language.
- **1.4.10 Reflow (flowchart diagram).** The diagram is a two-dimensional chart and needs scrolling in both directions on a narrow screen, which the criterion allows for diagrams. Mitigation: the text version of every page lists each box and where its arrows lead, and is the default on touch screens.
- **2.5.5 Target size (inline glossary terms).** Term buttons sit inside sentences and take the size of the text, which the criterion allows for inline targets. Mitigation: under a coarse pointer their hit area is extended by 8px above and below.
- **1.4.8 Colour choice.** The app provides no colour picker of its own: the Artifact host's theme setting (light, dark or system) is the user's control, and the app's tokens follow it. Adding a second toggle would fight the host's.
